Our Story

A Legacy of Protection & Trust

Founded in 1998 by former military and law enforcement professionals, ERMS Security began with a simple mission: provide uncompromising security solutions built on integrity, excellence, and innovation.

From a small team of 12 dedicated security specialists, we have grown into a global security organization employing over 2,000 professionals across 20+ countries. Our growth has been driven by an unwavering commitment to our clients' safety and a culture of continuous improvement.

Today, ERMS Security is recognized as an industry leader, setting benchmarks for security standards, training protocols, and technological innovation in the protection services sector.
