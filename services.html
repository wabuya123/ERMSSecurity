Our Security Services

Comprehensive protection solutions tailored to your unique security needs.

Armed Guarding

Professional Armed Protection

Our armed guarding service provides highly trained, licensed security personnel who serve as a visible deterrent while being prepared to respond to any threat. All our armed guards undergo rigorous training exceeding industry standards.

  • Licensed and insured armed personnel
  • Rigorous background checks and psychological evaluation
  • Ongoing tactical training and certification
  • Site-specific security planning
  • 24/7 emergency response capability
CCTV Surveillance

AI-Powered Video Surveillance

Our state-of-the-art CCTV systems leverage artificial intelligence and machine learning to provide intelligent monitoring, real-time threat detection, and comprehensive video analytics.

  • 4K and thermal camera systems
  • AI-powered facial recognition and object detection
  • Remote monitoring via secure mobile app
  • Cloud and on-premise storage options
  • Integration with existing security infrastructure
Access Control

Intelligent Access Management

Secure your facilities with our advanced access control solutions, from biometric systems to mobile credentials. Centralized management provides real-time visibility and control over who enters your premises.

  • Biometric fingerprint and facial recognition
  • Smart card and mobile credential systems
  • Integration with visitor management
  • Real-time access reporting and audit trails
  • Scalable from single door to enterprise deployments
Alarm Response

Rapid Alarm Verification & Response

Our rapid response teams are strategically positioned to ensure the fastest possible response to alarm activations. With verified response protocols, we minimize false alarms while ensuring genuine threats are addressed immediately.

  • Average response time under 5 minutes
  • 24/7/365 monitoring and dispatch
  • Two-factor alarm verification
  • Direct police and emergency services liaison
  • Detailed incident reporting and analysis
Risk Assessment

Comprehensive Security Risk Analysis

Our certified risk assessors conduct thorough evaluations of your premises, operations, and existing security measures to identify vulnerabilities and recommend effective mitigation strategies.

  • Physical security vulnerability assessments
  • Cybersecurity and data protection review
  • Compliance gap analysis (ISO, GDPR, industry standards)
  • Threat and risk modeling
  • Detailed remediation roadmap
Executive Protection

Discreet Executive Security

Our executive protection specialists provide close protection services for high-profile individuals, corporate executives, and VIPs. Services are tailored to each client's risk profile, travel schedule, and lifestyle.

  • Close protection by certified specialists
  • Advance security planning and route analysis
  • Secure transportation and convoy management
  • Residence and workplace security
  • International travel security coordination
